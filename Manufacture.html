Our Story

A Family Atelier.
A Quarter Century
of Mastery.

In the quiet hills beyond Firenze, where the Arno whispers through ancient leather workshops, the Vonaldo family has been crafting footwear with a singular devotion for twenty-five years. What began as a father's promise to his craft has become a living testament to the most enduring tradition in Italian fashion — the art of the hand-stitched shoe.

Every pair that leaves our atelier carries with it the unhurried patience of a craftsman who refuses to compromise. We select only the finest full-grain Italian leathers — sourced from the tanneries of Santa Croce sull'Arno — and work each hide through no fewer than eighty individual steps. From the first cut of the upper to the final burnishing of the welt, each gesture is deliberate, each stitch placed with the precision of a maestro.

Today, as Vonaldo steps forward into a new era, we carry the soul of that first workshop into every design. Modern silhouettes rooted in classical proportion. Innovation guided by heritage. Tradition worn — not merely preserved — by men who understand that true luxury is never rushed. Fatto a mano. Fatto in Italia.

Marco Vonaldo
Founder & Master Artisan

What We Stand For

The Vonaldo Standard

01

Premium Italian
Leather

Sourced exclusively from century-old tanneries in Tuscany, our hides are selected grain by grain. Only the finest full-grain leather — supple, durable, and alive with character — earns a place in our atelier.

02

Hand-Stitched
Precision

No machine replicates the intelligence of a master's hand. Our craftsmen execute each stitch with a measured tension learned through years of discipline — eighty steps, zero shortcuts, one unwavering standard of excellence.

03

Timeless
Design

We design for decades, not seasons. Every Vonaldo silhouette is drawn with classical proportion and refined restraint — a shoe that belongs as naturally to 1999 as it does to today, and will still speak eloquently a generation from now.

"We do not make shoes. We make heirlooms — built to be worn, built to outlast, built to remember."
