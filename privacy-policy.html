privacy@vonaldo.com

At Vonaldo, your privacy is a fundamental part of how we operate. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We are committed to handling your data with the same care and precision we apply to every pair of shoes we craft.

01

Information We Collect

When you interact with Vonaldo — whether browsing our website, creating an account, or placing an order — we may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal identifiers: Full name, email address, phone number, and billing or shipping address
  • Payment information: Card type and last four digits processed securely via our payment provider — we never store full card details
  • Account data: Username, password (encrypted), order history, and saved preferences
  • Device & usage data: IP address, browser type, operating system, pages visited, and time spent on site
  • Communications: Messages sent to our support team, newsletter sign-ups, and feedback submitted

We only collect what we need. Vonaldo does not collect sensitive personal data such as government IDs, health information, or biometric data.

02

How We Use Your Data

Your information is used exclusively to provide and improve your experience with Vonaldo. Specifically, we use your data to:

  1. Process and fulfil your orders, including shipping notifications and delivery updates
  2. Manage your account and provide customer support
  3. Send transactional emails such as order confirmations and receipts
  4. Send marketing communications if you have opted in — you can unsubscribe at any time
  5. Analyse website usage to improve performance, content, and user experience
  6. Prevent fraud, unauthorised transactions, and abuse of our services
  7. Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

03

Sharing Your Information

Vonaldo does not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: Trusted partners who assist in payment processing, order fulfilment, shipping, and email delivery — bound by strict data agreements
  • Analytics providers: Anonymised usage data shared with analytics tools to improve our website
  • Legal requirements: When required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of Vonaldo or others
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger or acquisition, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction — you will be notified in advance

We never sell your data. Any third party we work with is contractually required to handle your information in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable data protection law.

04

Cookies & Tracking

Vonaldo uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience on our website. Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us recognise returning visitors and understand how our site is used.

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Essential cookies: Required for core website functionality such as your shopping cart and account login
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand traffic patterns and improve site performance
  • Marketing cookies: Used to deliver relevant advertising and measure campaign effectiveness
  • Preference cookies: Remember your settings and language preferences

You may control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of the website.

05

Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including legal, accounting, and reporting requirements.

  • Order and transaction data is retained for a minimum of 7 years for tax and legal compliance
  • Account data is retained for the duration of your account and up to 2 years after closure
  • Marketing preferences are retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion
  • Support communications are retained for up to 3 years

Upon request, we will delete or anonymise your personal data where we are not legally required to retain it.

06

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data. We are committed to honouring these rights promptly and without charge:

  1. Right of access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  2. Right to rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  3. Right to erasure: Request deletion of your personal data where no legal obligation requires us to retain it
  4. Right to restriction: Request that we limit how we process your data in certain circumstances
  5. Right to portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  6. Right to object: Object to processing of your data for marketing or profiling purposes
  7. Right to withdraw consent: Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@vonaldo.com. We will respond within 30 days.

07

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmitted through our website
  • Secure, access-controlled storage for all personal data
  • Regular security assessments and vulnerability testing
  • Strict internal access controls — only authorised personnel can access personal data

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we take every reasonable precaution, we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you suspect your account has been compromised, please contact us immediately.

08

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, social media platforms, or partner services. These sites operate independently and are governed by their own privacy policies. Vonaldo is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of any third-party websites.

We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any external site you visit before providing any personal information.

09

Children's Privacy

The Vonaldo website is intended for use by individuals aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 18. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected data from a minor, we will take immediate steps to delete that information.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at privacy@vonaldo.com and we will promptly address the matter.

10

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or the services we offer. When we make material changes, we will notify you by updating the effective date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, by sending an email notification.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Your continued use of the Vonaldo website following any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

11

Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or how we handle your personal data, please reach out to our dedicated privacy team:

  • Email: privacy@vonaldo.com
  • General support: support@vonaldo.com
  • Response time: Within 30 days for data rights requests, 2 business days for general enquiries

We are committed to resolving any privacy concerns transparently and promptly. Your trust is something we take seriously at every level of our business.
