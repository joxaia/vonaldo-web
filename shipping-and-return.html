At Vonaldo, every pair is prepared with care before it leaves our hands. We want your experience — from the moment you order to the moment your shoes arrive — to reflect the same standard of excellence we put into our craft. Here is everything you need to know about shipping and returns.

01

Processing Time

All orders are processed and dispatched within 1–3 business days of payment confirmation. During peak periods such as sales or new launches, processing may take up to 5 business days. You will receive a shipping confirmation email with your tracking details as soon as your order is on its way.

Please note: Orders placed on weekends or public holidays will begin processing on the next available business day.

02

Shipping Methods & Costs

We offer a range of shipping options to suit your needs. Estimated delivery times begin from the date of dispatch, not the date of order.

  • Standard Shipping (5–10 business days): Available on all orders. Free on orders over $200
  • Express Shipping (2–4 business days): Available at checkout for an additional fee based on destination
  • Overnight / Next-Day Shipping: Available for select destinations — contact us before ordering to confirm availability

Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on your location, order weight, and selected shipping method.

03

International Shipping

Vonaldo ships to most countries worldwide. International delivery times vary depending on the destination country and customs processing. Estimated timeframes are as follows:

  • Europe: 5–9 business days
  • Middle East & North Africa: 6–10 business days
  • North America: 7–12 business days
  • Asia Pacific: 8–14 business days
  • Rest of World: 10–18 business days

These are estimated delivery windows only and are not guaranteed. Delays caused by customs, public holidays, or carrier disruptions are outside our control.

04

Order Tracking

Once your order has been dispatched, you will receive a shipping confirmation email containing your tracking number and a link to track your parcel in real time. Tracking information may take up to 24 hours to update after dispatch.

  1. Check your confirmation email for your tracking number
  2. Visit the carrier's tracking page using the link provided
  3. Enter your tracking number to view live shipment status
  4. If tracking has not updated after 48 hours, contact our support team
05

Duties & Taxes

Prices displayed on the Vonaldo website are exclusive of import duties, customs fees, and local taxes. These charges vary by country and are the sole responsibility of the recipient.

Important: Vonaldo has no control over customs charges and cannot predict or cover these costs. We recommend checking your country's import regulations before placing an order. Refused deliveries due to unpaid duties will not be eligible for a full refund — return shipping costs will be deducted.

06

Return Eligibility

We accept returns within 30 days of the delivery date, subject to the following conditions. Items that do not meet these criteria will be returned to you at your expense.

  • Item must be unworn, unused, and in its original condition
  • All original packaging, dust bags, and tags must be intact and included
  • Item must not show any signs of wear, creasing, or sole contact with surfaces
  • Sale items and final-sale products are non-returnable unless faulty or incorrectly sent
  • Custom or personalised orders are non-returnable unless faulty

Tip: We recommend trying your shoes indoors on a clean surface before wearing them outside. Outdoor wear immediately voids the return eligibility.

07

How to Return

All returns must be initiated through our Help Center before sending the item back. Unrequested returns may experience significant delays in processing.

  1. Contact our support team at support@vonaldo.com with your order number and reason for return
  2. Our team will review your request and provide a Return Authorisation (RA) number within 2 business days
  3. Pack the item securely in its original packaging, including all accessories and tags
  4. Write your RA number clearly on the outside of the parcel
  5. Ship the item using a trackable shipping method — we recommend insuring the parcel for its full value
  6. Share the tracking number with our team once dispatched

The cost of return shipping is the responsibility of the customer unless the item was received faulty or incorrectly.

08

Refund Process

Once we receive and inspect your return, we will notify you by email of the outcome within 3 business days. If your return is approved, your refund will be processed as follows:

  • Original payment method: Refunds are issued to the original card or payment method used at checkout
  • Processing time: Please allow 7–10 business days for the refund to appear in your account after approval
  • Original shipping fees: These are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error
  • Partial refunds: May be issued for items returned in a condition that does not fully meet our return policy

Please note: We are unable to issue refunds to a different payment method or account than the one used for the original purchase.

09

Exchanges

We currently do not offer direct exchanges. If you would like a different size or colour, we recommend returning your original item for a refund and placing a new order. This ensures you receive your preferred item as quickly as possible without waiting for the exchange process.

If you are unsure about sizing before purchasing, our team is happy to assist — contact us at support@vonaldo.com and we will guide you to the right fit.

10

Damaged or Wrong Items

In the rare event that you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery. We will resolve the issue promptly at no additional cost to you.

  1. Email support@vonaldo.com with your order number
  2. Attach clear photographs of the item and its packaging
  3. Our team will assess the issue and confirm next steps within 1 business day
  4. We will arrange a prepaid return label and send a replacement or issue a full refund

We stand behind every pair we make. If something is not right, we will make it right — no questions asked.

11

Contact Us

Our customer care team is available to assist with any shipping or returns enquiry. We aim to respond to all messages within 2 business days.

  • Email: support@vonaldo.com
  • Help Center: Available on our website
  • Response time: Within 2 business days

We are committed to making every part of your Vonaldo experience as smooth and satisfying as possible — from your first browse to your final delivery.
